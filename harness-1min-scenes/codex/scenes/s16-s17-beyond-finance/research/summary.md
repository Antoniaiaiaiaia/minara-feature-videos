# NVIDIA fiscal 2026 earnings

NVIDIA's fiscal 2026 ended January 25, 2026. Revenue reached **$215.938 billion**, up **65%** from fiscal 2025. Data Center revenue was **$193.737 billion**, up **68%**, and GAAP net income was **$120.067 billion**, up **65%**. GAAP gross margin was **71.1%**, down **3.9 percentage points** from **75.0%** in fiscal 2025.

The year was defined by AI infrastructure demand. Data Center revenue represented about **89.7%** of total FY2026 revenue. NVIDIA's 10-K says the margin decline reflected the transition from Hopper HGX systems to Blackwell full scale data center solutions, alongside a **$4.5 billion H20 charge** tied to excess inventory and purchase obligations.

Three short insights:

1. **Data Center carried the year.** AI and accelerated computing drove 68% Data Center growth, making the end market the central driver of NVIDIA's FY2026 result.
2. **Scale came with a margin tradeoff.** Net income still rose 65%, but gross margin fell 3.9 points as the product mix and H20 charge affected profitability.
3. **Concentration and China are the key watch items.** One direct customer represented 22% of FY2026 revenue and another 14%. NVIDIA also said it was effectively foreclosed from China's data center compute market at fiscal year end; its Q1 FY2027 outlook assumes no China Data Center compute revenue.

The figures above are GAAP unless labeled otherwise. NVIDIA's press release reports FY2026 non-GAAP gross margin of 71.3% and non-GAAP diluted EPS of $4.77, versus GAAP diluted EPS of $4.90. Beginning in Q1 FY2027, NVIDIA says its non-GAAP measures will include stock based compensation expense, which affects period to period comparability.
